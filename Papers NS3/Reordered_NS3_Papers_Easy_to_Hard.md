## Difficulty Classification Summary

- **EASY (1-10):** Simple parameter modifications, basic enhancements
- **EASY-MODERATE (11-20):** Single concept deep-dive (AQM variations, basic TCP mods)
- **MODERATE (21-34):** TCP variants requiring solid networking fundamentals
- **MODERATE-HARD (35-48):** Cross-layer, hybrid approaches, multiple mechanisms
- **HARD (49-63):** Advanced algorithms, datacenter/specialized scenarios
- **HARD-ADVANCED (64-75):** ML/RL integration, MPTCP, stochastic algorithms
- **VERY HARD (76-102):** Routing protocols (distributed, mobile, energy-aware)
- **EXTREMELY HARD (103-115):** Security, specialized analysis, complex protocols

## Recommendation for Selection

**For Beginners:** Choose from papers 1-20
**For Intermediate:** Choose from papers 21-48
**For Advanced:** Choose from papers 49-102
**For Expert/Research:** Choose from papers 103-115

Consider also:
- **Available NS-3 modules** (TCP modules are well-documented, some routing protocols have base implementations)
- **Interest area** (congestion control vs routing vs security)
- **Time constraints** (AQM modifications are faster than implementing new routing protocols)
- **Team size** (complex routing can be divided among team members)
